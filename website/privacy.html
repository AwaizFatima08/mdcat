Privacy Policy

MDCAT Prep by HomiLabs · Effective 10 November 2026

MDCAT Prep keeps your practice and mock results on your device. It has no accounts and we receive no data about you. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID; you can manage that choice in your Android settings.

Who we are

MDCAT Prep is published by HomiLabs. Contact: homilabs.smc@gmail.com.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your practice answers and accuracy by unit, your mistakes list and bookmarks.
  • Your mock papers: the answers you chose, flags, time used and scores.
  • A small counter used to space out ads.

This information is saved in the app's private storage on your phone or tablet. Other apps cannot read it. It is never sent to us.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows a full-screen ad from Google AdMob at a natural break: at most after every second practice set you finish and after a finished mock paper, and never more than once in three minutes. No ads are shown during a practice question, a practice set or a mock paper.

  • To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, app and device information, and ad interactions.
  • Where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the app asks for your consent before ads load, and you can change it later under About, then Privacy & ad choices. On Android you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in Settings, then Privacy (or Google), then Ads.
  • Your answers and scores are never sent to Google or anyone else.
  • How Google handles this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's Privacy Policy.

What we do not do

  • No account, sign-in, name, email address, phone number or precise location is collected by us.
  • We run no analytics or crash-reporting service of our own.
  • We do not sell personal data, and we receive none: ad data goes to Google, not to HomiLabs.

Children

The app is intended for MDCAT candidates aged 16 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Everything is stored only on your device. To remove it, uninstall the app, or open your phone's Settings, then Apps, then MDCAT Prep, then Storage, and choose Clear storage. We hold no copy of your results. Data held by Google for advertising can be managed in your Google account's ad settings.

Backups by your device

Android may include app data in your own device backup. That is controlled by your device settings and your Google account, not by HomiLabs.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
