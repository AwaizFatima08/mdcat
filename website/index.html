MDCAT at a glance

  • Paper: 180 multiple-choice questions in 3 hours, on paper with an OMR sheet. No negative marking.
  • Subjects: Biology 81, Chemistry 45, Physics 36, English 9, Logical Reasoning 9.
  • Difficulty: 15% easy, 70% moderate, 15% difficult.
  • Passing: 55% for MBBS and 50% for BDS. Admission is decided on merit, so every extra mark counts.
