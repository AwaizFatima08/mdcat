# Physics addendum to WRITER_BRIEF.md

The writer brief was written for Biology; for Physics, read it with these
changes. Where they differ, this file wins.

- Subject: FSc Physics (Pakistani boards, Part I and II) within the PM&DC
  MDCAT curriculum, `docs/sources/PMDC-Uniform-Curriculum-MDCAT-2025-Final.txt`
  section "3. PHYSICS". Use `content/questions/chem/chem-03.json` (Gases) as
  an example of numericals in house style.
- **Numericals:** MDCAT has no calculator. In LOs with formulas, about a
  third to a half of questions should be numericals whose numbers work by
  hand in under a minute: g = 10 m s⁻² unless the stem says 9.8; friendly
  angles (30°, 45°, 60°, 90°, with sin 30° = 0.5 etc. given or standard);
  k = 9 × 10⁹ N m² C⁻²; h = 6.6 × 10⁻³⁴ J s; e = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C; c = 3 × 10⁸ m s⁻¹.
  Give any data the student needs in the stem. Work every answer twice.
  Distractors are results of typical mistakes (forgot to square, used sin
  for cos, forgot the factor 2, wrong unit prefix).
- **Concept questions:** proportionality ("if the distance is doubled, the
  force becomes…"), graphs described in words (slope of a v–t graph), units
  and dimensions, and direction rules (right-hand rule, Lenz's law) are core
  MDCAT styles; use them.
- **Notation:** plain Unicode only, no HTML or LaTeX: m s⁻², 10⁻¹⁹, Ω, μF, λ,
  θ, ½, √2, v², T½, ×, →. Vectors in bold aren't possible: write "vector A"
  or use names like vector P and vector Q.
- **Letters:** the checker rejects "A and B", "(C)" etc. Name vectors P, Q, R
  and points X, Y; never use A–D as labels.
- **Conventions that vary between books:** avoid or word around them (e.g.
  sign conventions for work in the first law: say "work done on the gas" or
  "work done by the gas" explicitly; Cp − Cv = R per mole).
- `x` may include one short line of working for numericals.
