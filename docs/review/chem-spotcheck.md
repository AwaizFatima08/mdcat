_Zymase catalyses fermentation: C₆H₁₂O₆ → 2C₂H₅OH + 2CO₂. Splitting sucrose into glucose and fructose is done by invertase._

**chem-19-027** · CHEM-19.3 · M  
The enzyme invertase hydrolyses sucrose into:  
A) Two molecules of glucose  
B) Glucose and galactose  
**C) Glucose and fructose ✓**  
D) Ethanol and carbon dioxide  
_Sucrose + H₂O → glucose + fructose, catalysed by invertase. Maltose gives two glucose units, and lactose gives glucose and galactose._

**chem-19-029** · CHEM-19.3 · M  
The non-protein organic molecule that some enzymes need in order to work, often made from a vitamin, is called a:  
**A) Coenzyme ✓**  
B) Substrate  
C) Apoenzyme  
D) Zymogen  
_A coenzyme is an organic cofactor, often derived from B vitamins, that binds to the enzyme and helps it act. The apoenzyme is the protein part alone, and a zymogen is an inactive enzyme precursor._

**chem-19-030** · CHEM-19.3 · D  
Which statement about an enzyme-catalysed reaction is correct?  
A) The enzyme makes the reaction more exothermic  
**B) The enzyme is unchanged at the end and does not alter the position of equilibrium ✓**  
C) The enzyme is used up in proportion to the product formed  
D) The enzyme works equally well at all pH values  
_An enzyme is a catalyst: it is recovered unchanged and speeds the forward and reverse reactions equally, so ΔH and the equilibrium position stay the same. Enzymes have an optimum pH and lose activity away from it._

**chem-20-010** · CHEM-20.1 · D

**⚠ chem-20-016** · CHEM-20.2 · M

**chem-20-018** · CHEM-20.2 · M

**chem-20-022** · CHEM-20.3 · M

**chem-20-029** · CHEM-20.3 · M  
Which of the following is a condensation polymer?

B) Polythene

D) Teflon  
_Nylon-6,6 forms with loss of water as each amide link is made, so it is a condensation polymer. Polythene, PVC and Teflon are addition polymers of alkenes._

**chem-20-030** · CHEM-20.3 · D
