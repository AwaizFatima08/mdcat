# MDCAT — research for the prep app

Prepared 8 Nov 2026. Official facts come from PM&DC and UHS documents (read directly). Facts from news and prep sites are marked *(secondary)*.

---

## 1. What MDCAT is

- **Medical and Dental Colleges Admission Test.** Required for MBBS and BDS admission at every public and private medical and dental college in Pakistan (PM&DC Act 2022, s.17(2)).
- **PM&DC** (Pakistan Medical & Dental Council, Islamabad) sets the curriculum, structure and weightage, and runs registration at `mdcat.pmdc.pk`.
- **Provincial "admitting universities"** conduct the test, set up centres and declare results. In Punjab this is **UHS Lahore**; in KP it is **KMU**. Sindh, Balochistan and the federal area have their own admitting universities. The format must follow the PM&DC curriculum exactly.
  - Whether all provinces sit one national paper or each province sets its own from the same curriculum is reported inconsistently *(secondary)*. Either way, the app should follow the PM&DC curriculum, not one province's paper.
- **Same day nationwide**, once a year.
- **NUMS** (armed-forces medical colleges) runs its own MDCAT with its own table of specifications. It is a possible later add-on and is out of scope here.

## 2. Current format (MDCAT 2025, carried over to 2026)

Source: PM&DC *Uniform Curriculum MDCAT-2025 (Final, 26-05-2025)* and the UHS *MDCAT-2025 Information Guide*. A PM&DC public notice (19 May 2026) confirms the **2025 curriculum applies to MDCAT-2026**.

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Questions | **180 MCQs**, 1 mark each, total 180 |
| Duration | **3 hours** (UHS: 10:00 am to 1:00 pm) |
| Mode | **Paper-based**, OMR bubble sheet (carbon copy kept by the candidate) |
| Options | **4 options (A–D)**, one correct answer |
| Negative marking | **None.** A lightly filled or double-filled bubble counts as wrong. |
| Pass mark | **MBBS 55% (99/180)**, **BDS 50% (90/180)** |
| Paper versions | Several codes (A/B/C/D) with the same questions in a different order |
| Calculator | Not allowed (no calculators, log tables or rulers) |
| Validity | Score is valid for **3 years** (PM&DC Act s.17(4)) |
| Answer key | Released on test day. Challenges are open until 6 pm the next day. Result follows within about 7 days. |
| Bad items | An expert panel deletes ambiguous MCQs and marks are adjusted proportionally |

### Subject weightage

| Subject | % | MCQs | Share of a 3-hour paper |
|---|---|---|---|
| Biology | 45% | **81** | ~81 min |
| Chemistry | 25% | **45** | ~45 min |
| Physics | 20% | **36** | ~36 min |
| English | 5% | **9** | ~9 min |
| Logical Reasoning | 5% | **9** | ~9 min |
| **Total** | 100% | **180** | **1 min per MCQ** |

### Difficulty mix (official)
**15% easy · 70% moderate · 15% difficult.** The app's mock tests should use the same mix.

### How the format has changed (secondary)
- Up to 2020: 200 MCQs, provincial syllabi, 60% pass mark (2018 rules).
- 2021–22 (PMC era): computer-based, 3.5 hours, 210 MCQs, 65% pass mark; Logical Reasoning added.
- 2023–24: 200 MCQs, paper-based, Logical Reasoning cut to 3%.
- **2025 onward: the uniform curriculum, 180 MCQs, 3 hours, Logical Reasoning back to 5%.**

Past papers from before 2025 still work as practice material, but their totals and weightage no longer match. The app's mocks must use the 2025 structure.

## 3. MDCAT 2026: what happened

- Originally set for 15/16 Aug 2026. On the Prime Minister's direction it was **rescheduled to Sunday 20 Sep 2026** (PM&DC press release).
- Registration was extended to 13 Jul 2026 (regular fee) and 18 Jul 2026 (late fee) (PM&DC notice, 8 Jul 2026).
- About **136,000 candidates** nationwide *(secondary)*.
- Punjab (UHS): result out on 24 Sep. 47,475 appeared; **79.43% passed for MBBS** and 85.22% for BDS *(secondary, Samaa/Express)*.
- KP (KMU): 69.17% passed for MBBS and 75.33% for BDS *(secondary)*.
- **The next exam is MDCAT 2027, expected Aug–Oct 2027.** The syllabus for it has not been announced. Watch pmdc.pk around Mar–May 2027.

**Implication:** with a 55% pass mark and about 70–80% passing, passing does not set students apart. **Merit does.** Getting into a public college needs roughly 160+/180 *(typical, secondary)*. The app should sell "raise your score", not "pass the test".

## 4. Merit (why the score matters)

- The usual aggregate is **MDCAT 50% + FSc/HSSC 40% + Matric/SSC 10%** (UHS formula, scaled to 1100 marks). Private colleges and other provinces vary slightly *(secondary)*.
- An **aggregate calculator** is an easy, popular feature to include.

## 5. Syllabus (official PM&DC 2025 curriculum)

The curriculum lists **units, then topics, then learning outcomes (LOs)**. The LOs use verbs such as "describe", "explain", "calculate", "analyse" and "compare". It is a **subset** of the FSc content, not the whole FSc books, so it is narrower than any one board's textbooks. Build the content around these LOs.

### Biology: 16 units (81 MCQs)
1. Acellular Life (viruses, HIV/AIDS)
2. Bioenergetics (respiration of proteins and fats compared with glucose)
3. Biological Molecules (water, carbohydrates, proteins, lipids, RNA, conjugated molecules, DNA structure, genes)
4. Cell Structure & Function (plant and animal cells, prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells, organelles, chromosomes)
5. Coordination & Control (receptors, neurons, impulse, reflex arc, brain)
6. Enzymes (characteristics, mechanism, factors, inhibitors)
7. Evolution (origin of life, Lamarckism, Darwinism)
8. Reproduction (human reproductive systems, menstrual cycle, STDs)
9. Support & Movement (cartilage, bone, muscle types, muscle ultrastructure and contraction, joints, arthritis)
10. Inheritance (Mendel, linkage and crossing over, sex-linkage, haemophilia)
11. Circulation (heart, cardiac cycle, blood vessels, lymphatic system)
12. Immunity (specific defence)
13. Respiration (human respiratory system, gas exchange, smoking)
14. Digestion (human digestive system and glands)
15. Homeostasis (kidney: filtration, reabsorption and secretion, stones, failure; thermoregulation; excretion)
16. Biotechnology (vaccines, probes, monoclonal antibodies, therapeutic products)

**Observations:** the syllabus is almost entirely **human and medical biology**. Classic FSc plant, animal-diversity and ecology chapters (kingdoms, plant physiology, ecosystems) are **not** in it. That is a big content saving and a selling point ("only what's in the syllabus").

### Chemistry: 20 units (45 MCQs)
1. Fundamental Concepts (moles, Avogadro's number, stoichiometry, limiting reagent, yield)
2. Atomic Structure (proton discovery, Planck's theory, quantum numbers, orbitals and their shapes)
3. Gases
4. Liquids (kinetic molecular theory, vapour pressure, H-bonding, anomalous behaviour of water)
5. Solids (crystalline solids, ionic crystals)
6. Chemical Equilibrium (Le Chatelier's principle)
7. Reaction Kinetics (rate law)
8. Thermochemistry & Energetics
9. Electrochemistry
10. Chemical Bonding (VSEPR, etc.)
11. s- and p-Block Elements
12. Transition Elements
13. Fundamental Principles of Organic Chemistry
14. Chemistry of Hydrocarbons (free-radical and other mechanisms)
15. Alkyl Halides (SN1/SN2, elimination)
16. Alcohols & Phenols
17. Aldehydes & Ketones
18. Carboxylic Acids
19. Macromolecules
20. Industrial Chemistry (dyes, etc.)

### Physics: 16 units (36 MCQs)
1. Vectors & Equilibrium
2. Force & Motion (graphs, projectiles, collisions)
3. Work & Energy
4. Rotational & Circular Motion
5. Fluid Dynamics (continuity, Bernoulli's equation, applied to blood flow)
6. Waves (including SHM and stationary waves)
7. Thermodynamics (first law, molar specific heat)
8. Electrostatics
9. Current Electricity
10. Electromagnetism
11. Electromagnetic Induction (Faraday, Lenz, transformer)
12. Alternating Current (including the EM spectrum)
13. Electronics (rectification, PN junction)
14. Dawn of Modern Physics (quantum theory, photons)
15. Atomic Spectra
16. Nuclear Physics (half-life)

**No calculator allowed**, so physics and chemistry numericals use easy arithmetic, round numbers and conceptual reasoning. The app's numerical MCQs must be solvable by hand in about a minute.

### English (9 MCQs)
1. **Reading & thinking skills**: scanning, meaning from context, figurative language
2. **Formal & lexical aspects**: vocabulary in context, synonyms (shades of meaning, irony and satire), pronoun–antecedent agreement, tenses, infinitives, gerunds, adverb position, prepositions, transitional devices, punctuation, sentence classes and phrases, inversion, active/passive voice, direct/indirect speech
3. **Writing skills**: proofreading and editing, faulty sentence structure, subject–verb agreement, usage and spelling errors

Typical item types: *find the error*, *choose the correct form*, *closest meaning in context*, *correct punctuation*, *voice/narration change*.

### Logical Reasoning (9 MCQs)
1. Critical thinking (true/false arguments, spotting false beliefs)
2. Letter & symbol series (arithmetic and geometric progressions, letter sequences)
3. Logical deductions (short passage, then pick the most accurate statement)
4. Logical problems (puzzles: seating, ordering, relations)
5. Course of action (statement, then which suggested action follows)
6. Cause & effect

This section is **not in any FSc textbook**. Students struggle with it, and good practice material is scarce, so it is a differentiator for the app.

## 6. Question style

- **Single best answer from four.** The stem is usually one line ("…is called:", "Which of the following…"). The UHS sample item asks which branch of biology is defined, with four field names as options.
- Many questions are **recall or "textbook line"** questions, but the official 70% "moderate" share and the LO verbs ("compare", "analyse", "justify") push toward **concept and application**: statement-based items, "which is NOT", comparisons, short calculations, and reading graphs and diagrams (displacement–time graphs, field lines, reflex arc, nephron).
- The syllabus is uniform, but students study **different board textbooks** (Punjab, Sindh, KP, Federal, plus the new National Curriculum books). Wording should follow the **PM&DC LOs**, not one board's sentences, so the app is fair to all students.
- **Pacing:** 1 minute per question with no negative marking, so the strategy is **attempt everything**. Students need timed drills and a practice OMR mode to build speed.

| KIPS, STEP (PGC) | Physical academies | Rs 45k–85k |

The market is **crowded and competes on MCQ counts**. Common gaps: answers checked against the 2025 LOs, quality explanations, offline-first use, Logical Reasoning practice, and honest progress tracking per unit and LO.

## 8. What this means for the app (for discussion, nothing locked)

1. **Content model:** every MCQ is tagged *subject, then unit, then LO code (e.g. BIO-15.2), then difficulty (E/M/D)*, with an explanation. Mocks are drawn at 81/45/36/9/9 and 15/70/15.
2. **Core modes:** LO-wise practice, unit tests, full **180-MCQ / 3-hour mock** with optional OMR-style answer sheet, a quick 10-question daily drill, a mistake review list, and weak-LO analytics.
3. **Extras:** aggregate calculator, countdown to the exam date, a coverage checklist for the official syllabus.
4. **Offline-first** (bundled question bank, like Urdu Safar). This suits students with poor data and keeps hosting cost at zero.
5. **Content is the real cost.** The work is roughly 270 LOs across 5 subjects. An original, reviewed question bank (Claude-drafted, then human-checked for factual accuracy) avoids copying copyrighted academy material or past papers. Past-paper "style" can be imitated but the questions should not be copied wholesale.
6. **Accuracy risk:** a wrong answer key in a medical-entrance app does real damage to trust. Every MCQ needs a review pass. A "report this question" button is cheap insurance.
7. **Timeline:** if the app should be useful for MDCAT 2027, it should launch by about **Mar–Apr 2027**, when serious preparation starts after FSc Part-II exams.
